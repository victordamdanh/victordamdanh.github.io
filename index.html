Leading WIPAC and inviting new teammates to build it with me is how I practice this every week.

Why the chessboard

Every lost position teaches the next move.

On a chessboard, a rushed move is usually the losing one. I learned that before I wrote my first line of code, and it still shapes how I work. When I design a system and the result isn't what I expected, I treat it like a lost position: I study what went wrong, find a new line and play again.

Even when a problem seems impossible or the budget is tight, I look for a smarter path before I consider giving up. The knight is my symbol because it never moves in a straight line, yet it can reach every square on the board.
